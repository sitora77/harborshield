## Files and transformations

`raw/page-*.json`: untouched response bytes, including source field metadata.
`singapore_daily.json`: selected source attributes, formatted as readable JSON.
No counts or dates were changed, imputed, removed as outliers or rescaled.
`provenance.json`: query URLs, pagination sizes, retrieval timestamp, checksums,
scope and revision note. These metadata are generated by the fetch script.
